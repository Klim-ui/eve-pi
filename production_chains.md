- **Cycle Time**: 16 hours per batch
- **For 4 units**: 64 hours = ~2.7 days continuous

## ⚡ Optimization Tips

### 1. Lava Planet Focus
All 8 chains depend heavily on Lava-sourced materials. Prioritize 1-2 Lava planets.

### 2. Parallel Extraction
Run extractors on all planets simultaneously to stock T0 materials.

### 3. Pipeline Manufacturing
Don't wait for all materials → Start T1 production as soon as raw materials arrive.

### 4. Export Timing
Coordinate launchpad exports with inbound deliveries to avoid bottlenecks.

### 5. Cycle Scheduling
Most chains are 16 hours. Sync cycles to end at same time for easier coordination.
